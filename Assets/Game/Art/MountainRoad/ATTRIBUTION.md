This level adapts the reference's road alignment and elevation into a simplified, stylized delivery environment. Changes include resampled terrain, smoothed and widened road geometry, adjusted grades, original low-poly trees and rocks, guardrails, road markings, signs, delivery bays and buildings, plus the pickup truck prop. The reference textures and full-resolution scan are not included. This attribution applies to the derived layout and terrain, including exported versions of this level; retain it when redistributing.

No separate overhead image was available in this session. A top-down projection of the supplied GLB was used to trace the route.

The delivery truck in the level is the project's separate **Pickup** model (`Assets/Game/Art/Pickup Truck`). It is not part of the Metazeon reference and is not included in the exported GLB; its source and license are those of the pack it came from.
